⚠ WHAT THIS COSTS

A gradient bar is colour carrying meaning on its own, which the rest of this app refuses to do — logged and missed are told apart by treatment, filled versus outline, precisely so state survives greyscale.

The marker is what saves it. Position is readable without colour at all. If the ramp ever ships, the number stays beside it and the marker does the work — the gradient is mood, not data.

⟳ REACTION · CUE TO ACTION 90 DAYS ▾
NOT ENOUGH EVIDENCE YET

Needs 5 days where the reminder fired and you logged, and 5 where it fired and you did not, inside 90 days.
Currently 7 / 5 and 1 / 5.

This measures the gap between the 7:50 notification firing and a session being logged — the exact interval the ten-minute floor exists to shorten, and the one Marx (2021) identifies as the aversive part.

It refuses to render until the evidence gate clears, which is Whoop's 5-yes-5-no rule and the same discipline as leaving K unset. Showing a trend from two data points would be the fake progress this project keeps refusing.

What to build, in order. Sidebar badges first — smallest change, seen every launch, uses counts that already exist. Then the irregularities table, because six red triangles you cannot read is worse than a list. Then Focus Flow, which shows you something true that the app has never surfaced. The ramp and Reaction last: the ramp puts meaning in colour alone, which this app has spent months refusing to do, and Reaction cannot honestly render for weeks yet. Both are right — neither is urgent.
