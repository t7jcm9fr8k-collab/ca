PICKING THE READ

S2 is the one to build. It is the only look descended from a mock you actually approved, it makes entering mean something (the frames you are staring at fill in), and it costs nothing per frame — outlines and text, no clock at all. S3 is the one you may actually want if the starting page should do a job rather than set a mood; say so and it is a straight swap. S4 is the best-looking and the first to wear out. S1 stays only if you like the silence. Any of them keeps the intent line and the 850ms power-on exactly as built.
