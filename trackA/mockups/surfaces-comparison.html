D

Flat black, rounded — the network monitor

Plain rounded cards on pure black. No cut corners, no hairline, no bloom. Separation comes from the card being lighter than the void, nothing else.

Costs: this is what every iOS app looks like. It is the one option where the HUD stops being recognisable as itself.

The question: which slab. The content, the spacing and the three state marks are identical in all four — so anything you like or dislike here is the surface, not the layout. The dark network monitor's density is available in every one of these; that is a separate decision from the slab, and it is the one worth taking from it regardless.
