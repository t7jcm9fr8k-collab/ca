What happens when a panel closes

Both grids below hold the same four panels. Close one and watch what the other three do. That difference is the entire decision — there is nothing else to it.

click again to reopen

REFLOWING — what most apps do

The survivors slide up to fill the gap. No wasted space, and nothing is where it was left.

LOCKED — what this app does

The slot stays empty. The gap stays visible, and every other panel is exactly where it was.

Reflowing costs muscle memory

Every open and close reshuffles the screen, so it has to be read to find anything. The layout is never the same twice, which means it can never become automatic. Space is saved and paid for in attention, every single time.

Locked costs space

The punch list is top-left at 3am and top-left at noon. After a week the screen no longer needs reading — a glance finds it. The cost is holes; what comes back is the thing that makes an instrument an instrument — the hand knows where the dial is.

Why this is a live question and not a preference: ⌘1–⌘4 already gives spatial persistence at the navigation level — destination 2 is always destination 2. The grid question is about what happens inside one destination. If panels there reflow, a stable outer shell wraps a surface that rearranges itself, which is the worst of both — the route is learnable, but the arrival still has to be searched.
